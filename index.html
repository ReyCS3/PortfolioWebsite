CAD Build Battles In progress

A round-based multiplayer Roblox game that swaps Minecraft's block palette for parametric modelling: players sketch on a plane, then extrude and revolve it into a solid, and get scored on the result. Sole developer — I design the systems and drive implementation through an AI coding assistant, then debug and integrate in Studio. Round state machine, plot allocation, a CAD-style camera, and mesh manipulation built on EditableMesh.

About me

I'm a builder and a problem solver at heart. As a Computer Science senior at CSULB, my interest sits where digital architecture meets physical engineering — and I'm happiest on projects that need both.

Whether I'm structuring a Firestore schema, dialling in a custom fabrication on my Sidewinder X1, or doing mechanical diagnostics on a '98 SL500, the appeal is the same: working out how a complex system actually behaves, then making it behave better.

My professional foundation is hands-on operational experience at Disney California Adventure. High-capacity, fast-paced environments taught me to troubleshoot under pressure, adapt on the fly, and work in a team where everyone is already busy. That transfers to engineering more directly than I expected it to.

Outside of work I run a self-hosted Purpur Minecraft server for friends, tunnelled through playit.gg after a hosted provider couldn't keep up. I also bowl in a league (still chasing a 200 average), tune my PC, and make music in FL Studio.
