CAD Build Battles In progress

A round-based multiplayer Roblox game that swaps Minecraft's block palette for parametric modelling: players sketch on a plane, extrude or revolve it into a solid, and get scored on what they make. Sole developer — I design the systems and drive implementation through Lemonade.gg, an AI assistant that generates Luau and syncs into Studio, then debug and integrate the result myself. The architecture is mine: the round state machine, plot allocation across players, a CAD-style camera that switches between orbit and plane views, and mesh manipulation built on Roblox's EditableMesh API.

  • Luau
  • Roblox Studio
  • EditableMesh
  • State machines
  • Multiplayer
